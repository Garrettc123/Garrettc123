# Legal Care & Compliance Shield

Garcar Enterprise — Private Legal Protection System
Owner: Garrett Carrol / Garcar Enterprise

## Purpose
This document is an internal governance baseline for enterprise systems, repositories, intellectual property, and AI operations. It is not legal advice and does not itself create legal compliance. Have qualified Texas counsel review it before relying on it externally.

## Controls
- Maintain an inventory of repositories, systems, data categories, owners, vendors, and access levels.
- Classify confidential information and trade secrets; limit access on a need-to-know basis; use written confidentiality and invention-assignment agreements where appropriate.
- Use explicit repository licenses. Unlicensed code should not be assumed to grant permission to others.
- Protect personal information with data minimization, documented retention/deletion schedules, access controls, encryption, and incident-response procedures.
- Track applicable privacy, consumer-protection, employment, tax, intellectual-property, security, and AI requirements with counsel.
- Maintain immutable security and change logs, risk reviews, vendor assessments, backup and recovery tests, and policy approvals.
- Review AI systems for safety, discrimination risks, transparency, data provenance, and human escalation paths.

## Governance
- Governing-law preference: Texas, subject to applicable law and counsel review.
- Review this policy at least annually and after material legal, product, data, or vendor changes.
